# AI gift card shopping — keyword research

Date: 15 November 2026
Market: Qatar first, GCC second, English and Arabic
Page under research: `/ai-gift-cards`

## Method and limits

This research used live search suggestions and public result pages:

- Google and Bing autocomplete for English and Arabic seed terms.
- DuckDuckGo HTML results for exact-match checks.
- Bing result pages for Qatar-specific commercial terms.

No paid keyword tool was available during this work (the web search integration
returned HTTP 402, and no Ahrefs/Semrush/Google Ads account was connected).
**There are no search-volume, CPC or difficulty numbers in this report.** The
findings are qualitative: they describe intent, competition shape and wording,
not traffic size. Treat the recommendations as hypotheses to confirm in Google
Search Console after launch.

## What the research found

### 1. "AI gift card" is dominated by generator intent

Autocomplete for `ai gift card` fills with `generator`, `maker`, `design`,
`creator`, `message` and similar. Searchers using this phrase mostly want to
*create* a card image or message, not to shop a marketplace.

Implication: do not target the bare phrase `AI gift card`. It would attract
people looking for a free generator and would misrepresent the page.

### 2. "AI gift card shopping" is an unclaimed phrase

An exact search for `AI gift card shopping` returned no DuckDuckGo results, and
Bing returned generic pages about AI and shopping rather than a matching
marketplace page.

Implication: the phrase is low-competition but also low-demand. It works as a
descriptive positioning phrase in the H1 and title, supported by stronger
commercial terms, rather than as the only keyword.

### 3. Qatar commercial terms are strong but brand-led

These queries returned commercially relevant results, dominated by banks,
telcos, malls and incumbent gift-card platforms:

- `buy gift cards qatar`
- `gift cards doha`
- `gift voucher qatar`
- `online gift cards qatar`
- `e gift card qatar`

Implication: these are the terms with real purchase intent. A new page will not
outrank the incumbents quickly, but the page should use this vocabulary
naturally, and internal links from the homepage and business pages should point
to it with descriptive anchors.

### 4. Arabic suggestions are sparse and brand-led

- `بطاقة هدايا` is broad and dominated by banks and large retailers.
- `بطاقة هدية قطر` surfaced little beyond `قطر مول`.
- `متجر هدايا قطر` returned no useful suggestions.

Implication: Arabic demand appears to be expressed through brand names and
generic gift words rather than a settled `AI gift card` phrase. The Arabic page
should read naturally for a Qatari customer and avoid literal translations of
English SEO phrases. Arabic keyword validation needs Search Console data, not
more autocomplete guessing.

### 5. Platform terms are volatile

Searches around ChatGPT and Claude connectors return news and help-centre pages,
not stable commercial results. Platform names are useful for clarity and trust,
but they are a poor primary keyword and a risky ranking target.

## Copy decisions driven by the research

1. The H1 is **"AI gift card shopping in Qatar"**, not "Buy gift cards through
   AI". It matches the positioning phrase without claiming that an assistant
   completes a purchase.
2. The title is **"AI Gift Card Shopping in Qatar | Marajukoo"**. The brand suffix
   is enough; no "buy through AI" promise.
3. Qatar and Doha appear in the lede, how-it-works, FAQ and related links, not
   stuffed into headings.
4. Arabic copy is written as customer language, not a translation of the English
   keyword list.
5. Platform names appear with an explicit "connector testing in progress"
   status, so the page cannot be read as a purchase claim.

Do not publish thin doorway pages. Each page needs real businesses, real
amounts, unique copy and internal links, or it will compete with `/ai-gift-cards`
for the same queries.

## Measurement plan

After launch, review in Google Search Console:

- Queries containing `ai gift card`, `gift card qatar`, `gift card doha`,
  `e gift card` and Arabic equivalents.
- Impressions and average position for the primary phrase.
- CTR on the title, especially whether `| Marajukoo` helps or hurts.
- Whether the page earns links from AI/MCP directories; those links matter more
  than on-page repetition for this niche.

Review after 28 days and again after 90 days. Replace this qualitative report
with volume-backed data once a keyword tool is available.
